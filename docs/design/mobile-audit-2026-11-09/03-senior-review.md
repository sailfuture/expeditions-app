# Senior UX/UI review — mobile audit

Reviewer: senior UX/UI. Inputs: `junior-planner-audit.md`, `junior-pages-audit.md`. Code checked against the working tree on 2026-11-09 (repo `C:\Users\hunte\GitHub\expeditions-app`). No source files changed. `path:line` is relative to the repo root.

## Verdict in five lines

1. Both audits are sound. Every P0 I checked is real; the planner junior's three corrections to the brief (no dnd-kit in the Weekly Planner, 5-day grid, `/dashboard` does not use the calendar/stats/map components) are all correct. Line numbers in the planner audit drift by ~30 lines in `planner/page.tsx` and the pages audit mis-cites `date-navigation.tsx` (file is 176 lines; the `hidden sm:flex` chevrons are at `:118` and `:158`, not `:249/:289`), but the substance holds in every case.
2. The single most valuable fix in the whole report is on Add Scores: a phone cannot change day except via the calendar popover, and Submit goes grey with no visible reason. Both are one-line class changes plus a 30-line bottom bar. Do that first.
3. Do not build the proposed phone Weekly Planner. On a phone it is the Daily View with a day strip; send phones to `/schedule/{date}` and spend the effort making the Daily View a legible list with a "day info" row. Keep the 5-column grid for `lg+` and go to 3 columns on tablets.
4. Trip Planner on phones should be read-only cards that open the existing day sheet. The bulk "Generate / Update Dish / Update Galley" buttons need confirmation on every device; that is a data-safety bug, not a mobile one.
5. Architecture: CSS breakpoints by default (`md` = phone/tablet line, `lg` = "has a pointer"), one small `useIsPhone()` for component-choice cases only, no generic `ResponsiveTable`, a top scrollable labelled tab strip rather than a phone bottom tab bar (the on-deck pages need the bottom edge for their own actions). Also: nobody noticed the app has no `viewport-fit=cover`, so every `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` suggestion in both reports currently evaluates to 0 on iPhones.

## Layout critiques

### Weekly Planner (`/planner`)

**Junior's proposal:** a new `PlannerAgenda` under 768px: collapsed date bar, Schedule/Galley segmented control, 7-chip day strip, day-summary card, agenda rows, bottom prev/+/next bar, stacked meal cards for Galley.

**My judgement: don't build it.** Lay the proposal next to what the Daily View already has (`schedule-view-client.tsx`): date navigation with calendar popover, a fixed bottom bar with prev / big + / next (`:1350-1382`), the item detail dialog, the Add Activity sheet, recipe thumbnails and the meal plan sheet (`:206-271`, `RecipeThumb`). The agenda proposal is that page with (a) a list instead of a timeline, (b) a day-info row, (c) a day strip. Items (a) and (b) are already on the Daily View's own fix list. The day strip is nice-to-have, and it belongs on the Daily View too if anywhere. Building a second one-day phone surface means two detail dialogs, two add flows and two bottom bars to keep in sync, which is how the three `formatMilitaryTime` copies happened.

What to do instead:
- Phone (`< md`): the Weekly tab's href stays, but the page `router.replace`s to `/schedule/{centerDate}?expedition=…` (or, if you prefer no redirect, the wall becomes a single large "Open today's Daily View" button). The redirect is better: a phone user who taps "Weekly" wants today, not an explanation.
- Tablet (`md`–`lg`): 3 columns (`[center-1, center, center+1]`), `line-clamp-2` titles, visible card actions (see architecture, hover-reveal), `min-h-[88px]` header instead of fixed `h-[88px]`, the Dish/Galley team lists in a tap-friendly `Popover` instead of `Tooltip`. This is the junior's W2/W3/W4/W5 set and it is right.
- Desktop: unchanged, plus W6 (error state) because an offline planner that offers "Add Template" on top of an existing day is a data problem, not a layout one.
- Galley view on a phone: covered by the Daily View showing meals with recipe thumbs; recipe *swapping* stays a tablet/desktop task. Do not port the galley columns.

### Trip Planner (`/dashboard`)

**Junior's proposal:** phone card list (date, A/S/O word, port → destination, staff off, notes snippet), bulk actions behind a `⋯` menu with `AlertDialog`, day sheet as a bottom sheet with an "Edit day" section (segmented A/S/O, selects, staff checklist, notes), group-by-location as sticky section headers.

**My judgement: right shape, trim the edit section until someone asks for it.** The phone job here is scan + jump. Every inline control in the table writes to Xano on first tap (`handleTypeChange :659-693`, verified pattern), so on a phone the read-only card list is not just nicer, it is safer. The day sheet already exists; make it open from the card, append `?expedition=` to its two navigation buttons (D7), and fix its footer so the two labels do not clip. Defer the "Edit day" section: the junior's own open question 2 ("who edits, and when?") is unanswered, and the current product memory says the planner is set up pre-trip. If watch leaders turn out to change A/S/O from phones, add the segmented control then; it is a contained addition to a sheet that already exists.

Two things to do on every device, not just phones: `AlertDialog` on Generate / Update Dish / Update Galley (D2), and an error state instead of "No schedules found" (D5). Those two together are the difference between "offline for a minute" and "someone regenerated the galley rota".

Tablet: the junior offers "drop `table-fixed`, `min-w-[880px]`, let it scroll" or hide Notes/Time Off. Take the second; a scrolling table of live controls is still a scrolling table of live controls.

### Add Scores (`/evaluate/[date]`)

**Junior's proposal:** full-width date button with the day-type word, an overflow `⋯` for Unblock all / Unlock all / Reason categories / View schedule, a fixed bottom bar (prev · Submit (N) · next) with the "N reasons needed" text in the bar and Submit kept tappable so the existing toast explains, 44px targets on Ban/lock/exclude, 48px stepper rows.

**My judgement: correct, and it is the top of the whole list.** This page feeds the daily cash award, so my only additions are about correctness, not polish:
- Keep `missingReasons.length > 0` out of `disabled` (the guard in `handleSubmitScores :398-407` already produces a better message than a grey button ever could), but keep every other disable condition (`lockingPeriod`, out-of-range, loading).
- "Unblock all" must get an `AlertDialog` on all sizes, a non-`X` icon, and a home in the overflow menu on phones. It silently turns N/A into a scored 3 for every student (`:352-366`), which changes the award. On desktop it is also two 40px icon buttons away from Submit.
- The bottom bar must not be covered by toasts. `app/layout.tsx:49` sets `<Toaster position="bottom-right" />`; on a phone sonner renders over the fixed bar, and the error toast is the only feedback the user gets for a missing reason. Move toasts to `top-center` (or position by `useIsPhone`).
- A sticky submit creates a new risk: a submitted day on a flaky link. The loop at `:412+` is sequential and stops at the first throw (pages junior Q10). Phase 1 should at least collect per-record failures and report "12 of 15 saved; retry 3", not a single error toast. That is a product correctness item, and I would do it before the 44px polish.
- Stepper `h-10` → `h-11` and the Ban pseudo-hit-area are correct and cheap. Agree with `md:grid-cols-2` staying; a 768 tablet in portrait is comfortable at two columns.

### Daily View (`/schedule/[date]`)

**Junior's proposal:** wrap the header so the date never truncates; chips in a collapsible "Day info" row; at `< md` replace the absolutely-positioned timeline with a `ScheduleRow` list (reuse `today-schedule.tsx:405-435`); bottom bar gains "Score this day"; FAB on an empty day offers "Add activity / Apply template".

**My judgement: agree, with one guardrail.** The timeline's value on a 375px screen is close to zero: two concurrent items give ~140px cards with 11px and 7px text (`draggable-schedule-item.tsx:236-294`, verified), and nothing in the page scrolls to "now" (`grep` for `scrollIntoView`/`scrollTo`/`now` returns nothing). A list wins on legibility. The guardrail: a list loses the one thing a timeline gives for free, which is a sense of "where in the day are we". Add a "Now" divider between the past and next items and scroll to it on mount when the date is today. Keep the timeline at `md+` where drag editing lives; the `editMode` toggle is already `lg`-only, so the list never has to support drag.

Also agree: location on phones (`:692` `hidden lg:flex` means a tablet cannot see "where are we" either) and the template picker inside the empty state. The bottom bar should pick up "Score this day" as a fourth button; four 48px round buttons fit in 375px with 16px gaps.

## Architecture decisions

**Shared `ResponsiveTable`/card primitive vs per-page fixes.** Do not build a table-to-card transformer; auto-generated cards from table columns are always the wrong card. Default every admin list to the galley pattern already in the repo (`table-fixed` + `%` widths + `hidden md:table-cell` + `px-3 md:px-6`, e.g. `inventory/page.tsx:900-909`), which is a class-level change per table. Build one small presentational `RowCard` (avatar/colour, title, two secondary lines, trailing badge, chevron, `min-h-[64px]`, whole card tappable) and hand-map it only for the four lists phones actually use on deck or at a dock: discipline records, awards summary, student evaluations, passport manifest.

**`useIsPhone` hook vs pure CSS.** CSS first: it is SSR-correct, needs no "mounted" flag and the repo has no media hook today (`hooks/` holds only `use-as-ref`, `use-isomorphic-layout-effect`, `use-lazy-ref`). Add exactly one `useIsPhone()` (`matchMedia("(max-width: 767px)")`, returns `undefined` until mounted) and use it only where the *component* differs: `Sheet side`, Dialog → bottom sheet, the planner redirect, Toaster position. Fix the breakpoint vocabulary while you are there: `md` (768) is the phone line, `lg` (1024) means "has a pointer and room for labels". The planner's `sm` and the header's `lg` currently disagree.

**Hover-only actions on touch.** The mechanism matters here: with Tailwind v4 the `hover:` variant is already `@media (hover: hover)`-gated, so `opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100` means permanently invisible on touch. Add `@custom-variant pointer-fine (@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine))` in `globals.css` and write `pointer-fine:opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100` wherever a control is hover-revealed (planner cards, draggable item pencil). Rule for the team: hover may *enhance* an action, never be its only path; on touch, a row tap opens and a visible `⋯` edits.

**Where the 13 tabs go on phones.** A top, single-row, horizontally scrolling strip with icon-over-label chips (56px wide, 48px tall, active tab scrolled into view on mount, breadcrumb collapsed to "‹ Expedition name"). Not a bottom tab bar: the two on-deck pages (Daily View, Add Scores) need the bottom edge for prev/next/Submit, and two stacked fixed bars is worse than scrolling. Not a "More" sheet as the primary fix either: it hides the Discipline and Awards tabs, which are on-deck jobs, behind a second tap. Fix the swapped Map/Route icons at the same time; with labels present it matters less, but the icons still carry the meaning at a glance.

## Things the juniors missed

1. **No `viewport-fit=cover`.** `app/layout.tsx` exports `metadata` but no `viewport`, so Next emits the default `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`. Without `viewport-fit=cover`, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` is 0 on every iPhone, so all of the `pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]` recommendations in both reports are no-ops until `export const viewport: Viewport = { width: "device-width", initialScale: 1, viewportFit: "cover", themeColor: "#ffffff" }` is added. The only place in the repo that already uses the inset is `app/public/passage-logs/page.tsx:2474`, and it is equally inert today.
2. **No web app manifest, no install path.** `metadata.appleWebApp` is set (`layout.tsx:28-31`) but there is no `app/manifest.ts` and nothing in `public/`. For a crew on a boat, "Add to Home Screen" is the cheapest connectivity win there is: standalone mode removes the browser toolbar (which also makes the `100vh` problem disappear) and makes the app feel like the tool it is. Add `app/manifest.ts` with `display: "standalone"` and the existing square icon. Offline caching is a bigger decision; the manifest is an afternoon.
3. **Tailwind v4 changes what "hover-only" means.** `package.json:90` is `tailwindcss ^4.1.9`; v4's `hover:` variant is wrapped in `@media (hover: hover)`. Every `opacity-0 group-hover:opacity-100` in the codebase is therefore *permanently* invisible on touch, not merely "awkward". The fix is on the `opacity-0` side (custom `pointer-fine` variant), as described under Architecture.
4. **Toasts cover the bottom bars.** `app/layout.tsx:49` `<Toaster position="bottom-right" />`. On a phone that is the same corner as the Daily View's fixed bar and the proposed Add Scores bar, and sonner's z-index is above both. The submit-error toast is the only explanation the user gets, so it must not land on top of the button they just pressed.
5. **`body { pointer-events: auto !important }` plus a 500ms poller.** `app/globals.css:124-126` forces body pointer events on, and `components/pointer-events-fix.tsx` polls for Radix's stuck `pointer-events: none`. Radix uses body `pointer-events: none` to stop interaction behind an open dialog or sheet; with it forced to `auto`, a tap outside a sheet both dismisses it *and* may land on the row underneath. On a desktop that is a mis-click; on a phone, where the sheet is full-width and the dismiss zone is small, it is a click-through onto a live control. Worth a five-minute test on a device: open the Add Activity sheet, tap just outside it over a planner card, see whether the card's dialog opens.
6. **The Planner and Map tabs have each other's icons.** `expedition-header.tsx:74-75`: Trip Planner → `Map`, Route Map → `Route`. On an icon-only strip (S1) the two most confusable glyphs are also the two that point the wrong way.
7. **No offline signal anywhere.** SWR is configured with `errorRetryCount: 2` and `dedupingInterval: 15_000` (`components/providers.tsx:14-16`); nothing reads `navigator.onLine` or shows a banner, and most pages collapse `error` into their empty state. A single `OfflineBanner` in the root layout (listen to `online`/`offline`, show "Offline — changes will fail until you reconnect") would do more for on-deck trust than any individual `LoadError` card, and should ship with #9/#12.
8. **No `hooks/use-mobile` exists despite the juniors assuming one might.** `hooks/` contains `use-as-ref.ts`, `use-isomorphic-layout-effect.ts`, `use-lazy-ref.ts` only. `useIsPhone` has to be written (see Architecture), and it should live there.
9. **The on-deck pages have no "now" anchor.** The Daily View renders a 16-hour timeline with no current-time marker and no auto-scroll (no `scrollIntoView`/`scrollTo` in `schedule-view-client.tsx`); Home's `TodaySchedule` list is similar. "What's happening now" is the single most common glance; both views answer "what happens today" instead. Covered by #7's "Now" divider for the Daily View; the Home card should get the same.
